What this is. A planning model, not financial advice — I'm not a licensed pension advisor (יועץ פנסיוני) and this doesn't account for your specific funds, insurance riders, or personal circumstances. It's built to answer "roughly, does the math work, and what moves the needle" — and to give you something concrete to take to an advisor.

How it calculates. Everything is in today's shekels (real terms), so inflation is already netted out of the return assumptions. Income tax uses the 2026 brackets including January's reform, with credit points for you and per child by age. Bituach Leumi and mas briut use 2026 rates and the 51,910 ₪ ceiling. Pension assumes 6% employee + 6.5% employer + 6% pitzuim; keren hishtalmut 2.5% + 7.5% up to the 15,712 ₪/month salary ceiling. Keren hishtalmut and gemel le'hashkaa are treated as accessible; pension is locked until 60 and then annuitised at your conversion factor. The Bituach Leumi old-age pension is 2,762 ₪/month for a couple, 1,838 ₪ for an individual, at official retirement age.

The big caveats. A single fixed return is a simplification — real markets deliver a sequence, and a bad decade right at retirement hurts far more than the average suggests. Tax rules, ceilings and the retirement age itself will change over a 20-year horizon. The model assumes your pension annuity holds its real value, which depends on your fund's returns beating its assumed rate. Treat the retirement age it gives you as a centre of gravity, not a date.
